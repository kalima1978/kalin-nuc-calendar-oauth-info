Privacy Policy — Kalin Gmail MCP

Kalin Gmail MCP is a private, owner-operated application for one Google account, accessed only with the owner’s consent.

Data accessed and purpose

The application uses the Gmail API gmail.modify scope for requested features: searching and reading messages and threads, reviewing email metadata and attachments, saving requested attachments locally, managing labels and Trash, creating drafts, and sending or replying to email. It does not ask for the Google account password.

Processing and storage

OAuth tokens are stored in private files on the owner’s computer. Gmail content is retrieved when needed for an owner-requested operation. Downloaded attachments may be saved under the owner’s local home directory. Drafts, sent messages, labels, and Trash operations affect the owner’s Gmail account. The app does not maintain a public database of Gmail content.

AI assistant processing

When the owner requests email help through a connected AI assistant, relevant Gmail data or tool results may be transferred from the private integration to that assistant provider to perform the request. The provider processes that data under its own terms and privacy controls. Gmail messages are not published on this website.

Sharing and limited use

The integration does not sell Gmail data, use it to serve advertising, or share it with unrelated third parties. Its use of Gmail API information follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access and deletion

The owner can revoke OAuth access in Google Account settings at any time. The owner controls their local OAuth files and downloaded attachments and can delete them.

Public website

This site has no analytics scripts or data-entry forms. The hosting provider may process normal access logs under its own privacy terms.

Contact

Contact the owner through the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
