Privacy Policy

Kalin NUC Calendar is a private, owner-operated integration with Google Calendar and Google Contacts.

Data and purpose

When authorized by the owner, the application accesses and modifies the owner's Calendar events and Contacts to perform requests. It does not request Google account passwords.

Storage and backups

OAuth tokens are stored in private files on the owner's own computer. Before making a contact change, the app saves a private local JSON backup of supported contact information. Backups remain locally until the owner removes them; this is not a full Google Takeout backup.

AI assistant processing

When the owner requests help using a connected AI assistant, data needed for that request may pass through the owner's private integration to the assistant service, subject to the owner's service settings and the service provider's privacy policy.

Sharing and control

The app does not sell user data or use it for advertising. The owner controls the data in their Google account and the local backups and tokens, and can revoke API access from Google Account settings.

Website

This public information site has no forms or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider may process normal access logs according to that provider's policies.

Contact

The owner can be reached through the support contact shown on the app's Google OAuth consent screen.
